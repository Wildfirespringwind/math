1. Show that $\alpha + \beta = \beta + \alpha$ for all $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$. <br/>
  Given $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$, let $\alpha = a+bi,\ \beta = c+di$.
  $\alpha+\beta = (a+c)+(b+d)i = (c+a)+(d+b)i = \beta+\alpha.$

2. Show that $(\alpha + \beta) + \lambda = \alpha + (\beta + \lambda)$ for all $\alpha, \beta, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$. <br/>
  Given $\alpha, \beta, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$, let $\alpha = a+bi,\ \beta = c+di,\ \lambda = e+fi$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\alpha+\beta)+\lambda
&= \big((a+c)+(b+d)i\big)+(e+fi) \\
&= (a+c+e)+(b+d+f)i \\
&= \big(a+(c+e)\big)+\big(b+(d+f)\big)i \\
&= (a+bi)+\big((c+di)+(e+fi)\big) \\
&= \alpha+(\beta+\lambda).
\end{aligned}
$$

3. Show that $(\alpha\beta)\lambda = \alpha(\beta\lambda)$ for all $\alpha, \beta, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$. <br/>
$\alpha, \beta, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$, let $\alpha = a+bi,\ \beta = c+di,\ \lambda = e+fi$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\alpha\beta)\lambda
&= \Big(\big(ac-bd\big)+\big(ad+bc\big)i\Big)(e+fi) \\
&= \big(ace-bde-adf-bcf\big)+\big(acf-bdf+ade+bce\big)i \\
\alpha(\beta\lambda)
&= (a+bi)\Big(\big(ce-df\big)+\big(cf+de\big)i\Big) \\
&= \big(ace-adf-bcf-bde\big)+\big(bce-bdf+acf+ade\big)i
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $(\alpha\beta)\lambda = \alpha(\beta\lambda).$

4. Show that $\lambda(\alpha + \beta) = \lambda\alpha + \lambda\beta$ for all $\lambda, \alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$. <br/>
$\alpha, \beta, \lambda \in \mathbb{C}$, let $\alpha = a+bi,\ \beta = c+di,\ \lambda = e+fi$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda(\alpha+\beta)
&= (e+fi)\big((a+c)+(b+d)i\big) \\
&= \big(ae+ce-bf-df\big)+\big(af+cf+be+de\big)i \\
\lambda\alpha+\lambda\beta
&= \big((ae-bf)+(af+be)i\big)+\big((ce-df)+(cf+de)i\big) \\
&= \big(ae-bf+ce-df\big)+\big(af+be+cf+de\big)i 
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $\lambda(\alpha+\beta) = \lambda\alpha+\lambda\beta.$
