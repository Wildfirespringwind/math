# chapter 1: Vectors
Given: $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$. Let $\alpha = a + bi$, $\beta = c + di$ where $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{R}$.

1. Evaluate $\alpha + \beta$

$$
\begin{align*}
\alpha+\beta&=(a+bi)+(c+di) \\
&=(a+c)+(b+d)i
\end{align*}
$$

2. Evaluate $\beta + \alpha$

$$
\begin{align*}
\beta+\alpha&=(c+di)+(a+bi) \\
&=(c+a)+(d+b)i
\end{align*}
$$

3. Real‑number addition is commutative: $c+a = a+c$, $d+b = b+d$.
   
$$
\beta+\alpha = (a+c)+(b+d)i = \alpha+\beta
$$

**Conclusion**: $\boldsymbol{\alpha+\beta=\beta+\alpha}$. The commutative law for complex‑number addition holds.
